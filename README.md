# HandSign Live 🤚✨  
A real-time hand-gesture recognition web app that converts your hand signs into words or speech — instantly. It’s fast, simple, and built to be extended.

## 🚀 Live Demo  
👉 https://aortal-spinosely-keaton.ngrok-free.dev

---

## 🌟 What This App Can Do  
- 🤳 **Real-time gesture detection** using your camera  
- 🟩 **Hold-to-confirm system** for stable gesture recognition  
- 🔊 **Auto-speak or send** when your hand leaves the frame  
- ⚙️ Adjustable **hold time**, **idle time**, and **send interval**  
- 🧼 Clean, minimal UI that keeps the focus on the gestures  

---

## 🎯 Why I Built It  
I wanted a smooth, practical way to turn hand signs into instant communication. Something simple enough to demo, but real enough to build on — whether it’s accessibility tools, gesture-controlled interfaces, or cool experimental projects.
